Adaptive Paddleboard

Accessible Design for Cerebral Palsy

In collaboration with KidsAbilitySept 2024 - Dec 2024
Mechanical PrototypingHuman-Centered Design
  • Collaborated with KidsAbility to conduct human factors research, conceptualizing and designing a novel paddleboard support system for teenagers with mild spastic cerebral palsy.
  • Engineered a kneeling-centric design alternative to traditional seated models, developing an adjustable, cushioned pole framework that improves core engagement, balance, and independent mobility.
  • Modeled the mechanical framework in SolidWorks, iterating on the design's center of gravity and structural geometry to mitigate capsizing risks and ensure dynamic stability on the water.
  • Fabricated medium-fidelity physical prototypes utilizing ergonomic foam padding and structural materials to validate boarding ease, safety constraints, and user comfort through practical physical testing.
